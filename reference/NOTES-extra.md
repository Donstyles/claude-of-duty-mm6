# Additional MM6 reference — screens pasted inline

Three screens the file set may not cover, recorded directly from images the user
pasted into the session. Merge into REFERENCE.md.

## A. CREATE PARTY screen

Four character columns filling the full width, framed in the usual fluted stone
columns.

- **Panel material: dark green serpentine/verd-antique marble** with black
  veining — *not* the grey granite used on the character sheet. This is a
  distinct surface and a remake will get it wrong by reusing grey stone.
- Title `CREATE PARTY` centred at top: large, white, wide-letterspaced serif
  caps on a grey marble bar with a thin gold rule.
- Each column header carries a **strip of blue sky with cumulus cloud** at the
  very top — a small painted vignette, not a flat colour.
- **Portrait**: oval, gold double-rim, painted photo-real head-and-shoulders on
  a neutral ground. Beside it a **gold/coloured class emblem**: blue heater
  shield with a white dragon (Paladin), gold longbow (Archer), gold ankh
  (Cleric), green broadleaf tree (Druid). Emblems are painted with highlight
  and shadow, not flat icons.
- Left/right chevron arrows in a recessed stone box, top-right of each column,
  for cycling portrait.
- **Name field**: recessed stone plaque, white serif, centred.
- **Stat block**, left-aligned label + right-aligned value, in a serif with
  generous line spacing. Colour encodes deviation from the class norm:
  - **green** = raised above base (e.g. `Might 25`)
  - **red** = reduced below base (e.g. `Luck 5`)
  - **white** = unmodified (e.g. `Endurance 11`)
  Order: Might, Intellect, Personality, Endurance, Accuracy, Speed, Luck.
- Small **left/right arrow glyphs appear beside the stat currently hovered**
  (visible on Intellect in the Druid column) for spending bonus points.
- `SKILLS` header in **gold caps**, then the class's starting skills: the two
  fixed skills in white, the two player-chosen ones in **green**.
- Bottom strip, three recessed panels:
  - `CLASS` — the nine class names in a 2-column list, gold header; the
    selected class in **cyan**, others white.
  - `Available Skills` — 3-column list, gold header, selectable entries in
    **cyan**, others white.
  - `Bonus Pts` with a `−` and `+` stepper flanking the number, above a blank
    plaque, above a gold oval button.
- **Flaming torches/braziers at the bottom-left and bottom-right corners** —
  animated flame sprites. A small but very characteristic detail.

## B. Skills panel (shown in the German build)

Same layout family as the character sheet.

- Background: **grey granite slab** with two large recessed sub-panels,
  bevelled with a light top-left / dark bottom-right edge.
- Header row: character name left, `Skill Points: N` right, both in **gold
  italic serif**. The number is gold.
- Left sub-panel: `Weapons` then `Magic` category headers in **gold**; right
  sub-panel: `Armour` then `Miscellaneous`. Each category has a right-aligned
  `Level` column header in gold.
- Each row: skill name in white serif, **mastery tier in italic immediately
  after the name** (Expert/Master/Grandmaster), level as a right-aligned
  numeral. One row is **red** — an unspendable/at-cap entry.
- Row of **five gold oval buttons** across the bottom: profile head (stats),
  fist (skills), crossed-out bell (awards/inventory), medal on a stand
  (awards), and a door-with-arrow (exit). Each glyph is a **dark silhouette
  embossed into the gold oval**, with a highlight arc across the top of the
  oval.
- Status line beneath in white italic: *"You need 4 skill points to advance"*.
- **Right column: full-body character render** standing in a dark stone niche —
  hand-painted, dramatically lit from the front-left, holding their weapon,
  wearing their actual equipment. Beneath it a **magnifying glass** icon.
  Above/right a small **search/inspect** icon.
- Food (**red apple**) and gold (**coin stack**) counters sit in a recessed bar
  just above the bottom portrait strip: apple then a number, then a larger gold
  number, then the coin pile.

## C. SPELLBOOK — the single most distinctive panel

- Rendered as a **physical open book in perspective**, with the page block
  curving away at the spine and the outer page edges visible as stacked leaves.
  The cover behind the pages is **dark green cloth/leather**.
- **Pages are cream/ivory** (`#e6dfc9`-ish), *not* brown parchment, with a very
  soft warm gradient and a subtle mottle. Clean, not burnt or torn.
- Spells are a **3 × 4 grid of hand-painted miniature illustrations** — each a
  little watercolour/gouache scene, not an icon: a mermaid in a wave, a rooster
  at sunrise, an ice-encrusted beam, a poison flask with a skull, a figure
  walking on water, a stone gateway, a night sky with a beacon. Each sits on a
  soft oval shadow with **no frame**.
- **Spell name beneath each illustration** in dark brown/black serif, centred,
  wrapping to two lines when long.
- The **currently selected spell is duplicated large in the top-left cell**
  inside an **ornate gilt-corner border** — this is the "open to this page"
  affordance.
- **Nine school tabs run vertically down the right edge of the book**, drawn as
  small ivory tab flags each carrying a **dark sigil**: a flame, a cloud/stone,
  a droplet, a rock, a robed figure, a head, a body, a compass star, a dark
  star. The active school's tab is pulled proud of the others.
- Two small buttons at the **bottom-right of the book block** (page turn /
  close).
- The instruction line in the grey marble bar beneath reads
  *"Select Town Portal"* — white italic serif, centred.

### Right-hand panel details confirmed here
- **Automap**: pointed-arch window with a carved stone frame; a compass plaque
  above it showing the facing letter (`S` here, `N` elsewhere) between two
  dots; `−` and `+` zoom controls at the arch's shoulders. The map renders
  **green terrain, red-brown roads, small green/white building blocks, and a
  white arrow for the party**.
- Below it, **two portrait frames side by side** holding **hireling NPC
  portraits** (painted female faces here). These are the two hireling slots —
  when empty they show the stained-glass windows seen in other shots.
- Then a **blank recessed plaque** (the message/name strip).
- Then **four book spines** in dark red-brown leather with **gold emblems**:
  dagger, feather/leaf, globe, key — these are the four "book" buttons
  (spellbook, notes/journal, map/atlas, quests/keys).
- Then the **food and gold readouts**: red apple + `6`, gold `36097` + coin
  stack, in gold numerals on a recessed strip.
- Then **four large gold oval buttons**: star, arch/temple, `2`-like rune, and
  a floppy-disk (save) glyph.

### Bottom bar detail confirmed here
- Material is **light grey/white marble with grey veining**, quite pale.
- **Portrait ovals have a gold rim** and sit slightly proud.
- **A dead character's portrait is replaced by a painted gravestone in a
  landscape** — a lovely touch worth reproducing.
- Each character has **two vertical bars** to the right of the portrait in a
  recessed slot: **green = hit points, blue = spell points**. Dead characters
  show the HP bar empty/absent.
- A **small round indicator dot** sits above the bar pair — green when the
  character is in good condition.

## D. INVENTORY panel — note the absence of a paper doll

This screen corrects a common wrong assumption.

- **There is NO separate paper-doll silhouette with empty slot boxes.** The
  equipment display *is* the **full-body painted character render** standing in
  the dark stone niche on the right. Equipped items are painted onto that
  figure (here: a red-and-gold slashed doublet, hose, and a staff held in the
  right hand). You drag an item from the grid onto the body to equip it.
  A remake that builds a Diablo-style slot grid has already lost the identity.
- **Backpack grid** fills the whole left/main area: roughly **14 columns x 9
  rows** of square cells, drawn as **thin dark red-brown rules over a dark
  brown wood/leather ground**. The grid lines are subtle, not high-contrast.
- **Items are painted sprites occupying whole-cell rectangles** at their true
  relative size: a staff spans about 1 x 5 cells vertically, scrolls 2 x 1
  each, a potion bottle 1 x 2, a reagent posy 1 x 1. Items are laid out
  free-form wherever they fit, not auto-sorted.
- Beneath the grid, the same **row of five gold oval buttons** as the skills
  panel: head (stats), fist (skills), crossed swords struck through
  (inventory), medal (awards), door-with-arrow (exit).
- Panel title `Inventory` in **white italic serif, centred** on the grey marble
  bar above the portraits.
- A **magnifying glass** hangs at the lower right of the character niche.
- The niche behind the figure is **dark dungeon stone** lit from the front-left
  — the figure is clearly lit by a separate key light from the environment.
